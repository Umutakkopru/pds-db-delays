# Reference data

Small hand-maintained tables that the pipeline reads. They are tracked in Git (unlike `data/`).

| File | Content | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `school_holidays_bavaria.csv` | School holidays in Bavaria, first and last day (both inclusive), school years 2024/25 – 2026/27 as far as the data period needs them | Bekanntmachung des Bayerischen Staatsministeriums für Unterricht und Kultus, BayMBl. 2022 Nr. 747 (https://www.verkuendung-bayern.de/baymbl/2022-747/), read on 11 Nov 2026. In the announcement the summer holidays are listed at the start of the school year that follows them, as here. "Buss- und Bettag" (school-free day, the Wednesday before 23 November) is not in the announcement and was added by its rule. |
